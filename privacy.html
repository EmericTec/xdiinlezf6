Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What the app accesses

When you sign in, the app asks for:

  • Google Sheets: read and update your spreadsheets.
  • Google Drive: read-only access to your files.

How the data is used

The app runs only on the owner's computer. It reads and updates the owner's own files. It has no server and the developer collects nothing.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared, or sent to third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the developer email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
